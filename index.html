Awakening

You wake up in the woods tired, thirsty, and hungry with no memory of how you got here...or even who you are.

The forest is quiet... unnaturally quiet. Something deep inside tells you that remaining here is a mistake. You need water, food, shelter. Answers will have to wait, you're certain you're not safe here.

A Clearing

You stumble through the thick forest, your mind in a fog, pushing past branches and bushes. Suddenly the resistance ends as you fall into a clearing.

It isn't large but there's enough space to do more than catch your breath. You can rest here. With some work you might be able to create a camp.

A Stream

You recognize the sound now that you see it, a small stream runs next to the clearing. As long as you're nearby you'll never be thirsty again.
