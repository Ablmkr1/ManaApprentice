Awakening

You wake beneath unfamiliary trees tired, thirsty, and hungry with no memory of how you got here.

The forest is unsettlingly quiet. You cannot recall your own name but some instinct makes you certain staying helpless will get you killed. Answers will have to wait.

A New Home

The small fire crackles as you huddle in your crude shelter. For the first time since waking you're not fighting for survival.

With a safe place to rest your thoughts turn inward. Who am I? Why am I here? The fire has no response, answers are...out there...somewhere.

Camp Outskirts Explored

You've explored everything immediately around your camp. What was once a mysterious forest is now a network of well-known paths.

You now know the fastest routes and shortcuts to every nearby location, and can travel these well-known paths faster.

With your new knowledge, you notice an overgrown trail leading beyond where you've explored. Maybe this can lead to the answers you seek.

A Spark

As your fingers close around the torch, heat blums in your chest. It races down your are for blue heat flickers beneath your skin for one impossible heartbeat.

You do not know how you did it but despited the impossiblity your instincts say it was you.

Mana Awakened

The runes do not speak in words. They pull at something buried inside you, and something answers.

Power gathers at the edge of your awareness. Forgotten no more a power, your power, blooms in your chest once more.
